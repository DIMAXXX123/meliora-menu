Our Chef

OG
Oleg Golovan
Head Chef & Culinary Director
Every dish we create is a conversation between tradition and innovation — where the precision of Japanese craft meets the boldness of modern gastronomy.

With years of experience across Europe and Asia, Chef Oleg brings a unique vision to Meliora. His approach fuses the delicate art of Japanese cuisine with contemporary techniques and locally-sourced Mediterranean ingredients, creating a dining experience that is both refined and deeply personal.
